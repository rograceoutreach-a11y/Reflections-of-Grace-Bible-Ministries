Who We Are

A ministry built on grace,
moved by love

Reflections of Grace Outreach Ministries exists to be a mirror of God's grace in our community — a 501c3 nonprofit that believes in helping as a servant of God, meeting people where they are, lifting up the discouraged, and walking alongside families in every season of life.

We are Ministers (Servants) who encourage others to have faith, believe in the Goodness of God, and believe that all things are possible. We are a unique "ministry without walls" dedicated to helping you build a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

Through outreach, worship, and fellowship, we strive to make faith practical — feeding the hungry, encouraging the weary, and sharing the good news of Jesus Christ with open hearts and open hands.
