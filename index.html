Who We Are

A ministry built on grace,
moved by love

Reflections of Grace Outreach Ministries exists to be a mirror of God's grace in our community — we are a 501c3 nonprofit organization that believes in helping as a servant of God, meeting people where they are, lifting up the discouraged, and walking alongside families in every season of life. We are Ministers (Servants) that encourages others to have faith, believe in the Goodness of God, and believe that all things are possible. We are an Outreach Ministry with Fruits of the Spirit that reveal Jesus teachings in our lives.

Thomas E. Walker, MDiv. — Founder Denise Ford-Walker — Co-Founder

Through outreach, worship, and fellowship, we strive to make faith practical: feeding the hungry, encouraging the weary, and sharing the good news of Jesus Christ with open hearts and open hands.

Our Mission

To reflect the grace of God by serving our community with love, compassion, and the message of hope found in Christ.

👁️

Our Vision

A community transformed by grace — where no one walks alone and every person knows they are seen, valued, and loved.

🌟

Our Values

Faith, service, integrity, and fellowship guide everything we do.

Our Ministries

Every ministry is an opportunity to serve. Here are some of the ways we reach out.

🕊️

Community Outreach

Food drives, clothing giveaways, and hands-on support for families and individuals in need throughout our community.

🙏

Prayer Ministry

Standing in prayer with anyone who needs encouragement, healing, or hope — no request is too big or too small.

Bible Study & Worship

Gathering together online to grow in the Word, worship the Lord, and build one another up in faith and fellowship. Care & Encouragement
